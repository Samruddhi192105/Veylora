# Veylora: The Last Hour — Game Knowledge

## Setting
The player is trapped in an abandoned study and must investigate objects, solve puzzles, find keys, and unlock the exit before time runs out.

## Grandfather Clock
The grandfather clock has stopped at 11:45. The unusual time is an intentional clue. The player should inspect the displayed time and consider whether it is relevant to a numeric lock.

## Old Painting
The old painting may contain environmental or visual clues. Encourage the player to inspect its details and compare anything unusual with other objects in the room.

## Diary
The diary contains a message that provides evidence relevant to the locked drawer. Players should read it carefully and connect its wording with the clock clue.

## Bookshelf
The bookshelf contains a sequence clue. Players should look for an order or pattern rather than treating the books as unrelated objects. This clue is relevant to the safe.

## Locked Drawer
The locked drawer requires a numeric code. Its solution is associated with evidence from the grandfather clock and diary. The drawer contains a small key when successfully unlocked.

## Safe
The safe requires a numeric code and the small key obtained from the locked drawer. The bookshelf sequence provides evidence needed to solve the safe.

## Telephone
The telephone is an investigable room object. The player should inspect it for story clues or additional guidance if the game provides them.

## Exit Door
The exit requires the exit key. The player must progress through the room's puzzles and obtain the required key before escaping.

## Hint Policy
Give hints progressively:
- Level 1: Encourage observation without naming the solution.
- Level 2: Connect two relevant clues or explain the next logical step.
- Level 3: Give a direct puzzle-solving hint, but do not invent evidence.
Never claim an object was discovered, a puzzle was solved, or a key was obtained unless the game state confirms it.
